End-to-End Assistive Pipeline Architecture

1. Sensory Input: Front-mounted camera captures 640x640 visual frames; microphone captures 32 kHz environmental audio; human body pose tracked in real-time.

2. Branch Processing: YOLOv8n tracks objects and estimates looming hazards; PANNs CNN14 extracts 2048-D acoustic features; GestureLSTM recognizes stop/waving/pointing commands.

3. Transformer Fusion: Projects heterogeneous features to 128-D, applies 4-head self-attention with modality dropout, and pools cross-modal context.

4. Spoken Feedback: AlertEngine applies confidence filtering and 5.0s cooldown deduplication, dispatching high-priority alerts to the speech queue.
